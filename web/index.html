ETH Payment Demo Türkçe

A shop takes payment in Ether through a smart contract. Switch between people, create an invoice, pay it, and watch the blocks.

Local test blockchain (Hardhat, chain id …). The ETH here is fake and has no value. Latest block: …

1 · Who are you?

Each person is a wallet: an address plus a secret private key. Click one to act as them. Balances are read straight from the blockchain.

2 · Merchant: create an invoice

Calls createInvoice() on the contract. It costs a small gas fee, paid by the merchant.

Merchant: withdraw

Payments are held by the contract until the merchant pulls them out with withdraw().

    4 · Mini block explorer

    Every block stores the hash of the block before it ("parent"). Change an old block and every hash after it breaks: that is why the history can't be quietly edited. Click a block, then a transaction.

    Select a block to see what's inside.
